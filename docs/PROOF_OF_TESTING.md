## Limits of this evidence

- The verified mainnet evidence establishes an independent t2000 SDK lifecycle and deployed application paths through rating, refund, worker timeout claim, and owner rejection.
- The application task's transactions used the controlled private local harness. Slush OAuth, real Slush personal-message binding/signing, cancellation, and reconnect are not verified.
- World checks use the configured sandbox/staging environment; they do not establish production Proof of Human or legal identity verification.
- Automated, local database, and S3 checks are not substitutes for the separately stated deployed/browser/chain observations.
- This record does not claim a completed independent production security audit. The platform report records remaining dependency-audit findings and product limitations.
